    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: November 2025

    1. Information We Collect

    Nexus AI stores all your data locally in your browser using localStorage. We do not have a central database storing your personal chats, generated images, or voice recordings. All generated content remains on your device unless you explicitly download or share it.

    2. Third-Party Services

    To provide AI capabilities, we route your prompts to the following services:

    • Pollinations.ai: Used for free, unlimited text and image generation. Your prompts are processed by their API.
    • OpenAI / Google Gemini: Only used if you provide your own API keys in the settings. Your keys are stored locally and never sent to our servers.
    • Google Sign-In: Used for authentication. We receive your name and email address to create your local profile.

    3. Payments

    Payments are processed via Razorpay and UPI. We never see or store your credit card details. Transaction IDs and screenshots are used solely to verify your subscription status.

    4. Data Retention

    Your data persists in your browser's localStorage until you clear it via Settings > Data > Clear All Chats or Delete Account. Uninstalling the browser or clearing site data will also remove all Nexus AI data.

    5. Contact

    For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at support@nexusai.app.

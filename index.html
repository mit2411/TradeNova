ℹ️ About EstatePro

Version 20k · August 2026

EstatePro Pro is a complete real estate brokerage CRM built as a single‑file HTML application. It is designed for brokers, agents, and property managers to efficiently manage their business operations.

Key Features

  • Property inventory management with full CRUD operations.
  • Lead tracking and client relationship management.
  • Appointment scheduling and site visit tracking.
  • Deal and commission recording with analytics.
  • Agent performance dashboard.
  • Task management with priority and due dates.
  • Blog, testimonials, team, and messaging modules.
  • EMI and commission calculators.
  • Dark mode and bilingual (English/Hindi) support.
  • Firebase authentication ready (Email, Google, Facebook).
  • All data stored locally in your browser (localStorage).

Technology

This application is built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It uses Firebase for authentication (optional) and localStorage for data persistence. No external libraries are required.

Open Source

EstatePro is open source and can be customized freely. It is designed to be a starting point for your own real estate CRM needs.

❓ Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a property?

Click the "Add Property" button in the top bar or go to the Properties page and click the "+ Add Property" button. Fill in the details and save.

How do I mark a property as sold?

On the property card, click the "Sold" button. The property will be moved to the Sold archive.

How do I restore a sold property?

Go to the Sold page, find the property, and click "Restore". It will be moved back to active inventory.

How do I export data?

Use the "Export" buttons available on the Properties page (exports all properties) and on the Reports page (exports full backup as JSON).

How do I change the language?

Click the language toggle button (e.g., "EN") in the top bar to switch between English and Hindi.

How do I enable dark mode?

Click the moon icon in the top bar to toggle dark mode.

What if I lose my data?

All data is stored in your browser's localStorage. Do not clear your browser data for that site. You can also export full backups regularly.

Can I use this with a backend?

Yes, you can replace the localStorage functions with API calls to a backend server. The code is structured to allow easy integration.
